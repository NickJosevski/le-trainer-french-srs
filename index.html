Welcome to Le Trainer

Learn a language a few words at a time. Here's the whole idea:

  1. 1 Pick a language to start with.
  2. 2 Meet a new word — see it, hear it, learn what it means. No pressure.
  3. 3 Get quizzed later and tap how well you knew it. We handle the schedule for you.

A few minutes a day is all it takes. You can switch languages any time.

How it works

The daily loop

Meet a few new words, then review them on a schedule. Words you find hard come back sooner; words you know come back later. Just show up most days — even a few minutes helps.

Meeting a new word

New words appear as a learn card first: you see the word, hear it, and read what it means. Nothing to get wrong. You'll be quizzed on it in a later session.

Rating a word you're quizzed on

  • Again no idea / got it wrong
  • Hard right, but a struggle
  • Good got it
  • Easy too easy

Honest ratings make the schedule smarter. When in doubt, pick Good.

A few words you'll see

  • Cloze — a sentence with the target word blanked out ([…]) so you recall it in context.
  • IPA — the text in /slashes/ shows how the word is pronounced.
  • A1–C1 — difficulty (the CEFR scale). A1 is easiest / beginner, C1 is advanced.
